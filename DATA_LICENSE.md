This covers:

- `data/enpad/`: the ENPAD pipeline outputs (severity labels, risk scores and warnings);
- `data/human_studies/`: the anonymized human annotations and ratings;
- the label and box files in `data/sample/`.

You may share and adapt them for any purpose, provided you give appropriate credit
by citing the paper (see the main README).

from the public pothole dataset of Nienaber, Booysen and Kroon (2015). They remain
subject to that dataset's own terms and are not relicensed here.
